About Swamiji

A life of spiritual discipline and public service.

Swami Anandavanam Bharati Maharaj serves as a Mahamandaleshwar of Juna Akhada. Public reports from 2025 describe him as the first Mahamandaleshwar of Juna Akhada from Kerala.

Before his elevation as Mahamandaleshwar, he was publicly identified as the Managing Trustee of Kalika Peetham while coordinating Kerala’s organised participation in the 2025 Prayagraj Maha Kumbh. The effort included service initiatives for sannyasis and devotees.

His work in Kerala has included visits and dialogue with spiritual institutions, community leaders and service organisations, with an emphasis on spiritual practice, the preservation of Sanatana Dharma and constructive community activity.

In 2026, he was also publicly associated with the Mahamagha Mahotsavam at Thirunavaya and with proposals concerning the rejuvenation of the Bharathapuzha.

Public Milestones

Selected moments from his work in Kerala.

JAN 2025

Prayagraj Maha Kumbh

Kalika Peetham coordinated Kerala’s organised monastic participation and service initiatives at the Maha Kumbh. Contemporary reports identified Sadhu Anandavanam as its Managing Trustee.

JAN 2025

Mahamandaleshwar of Juna Akhada

He was elevated as a Mahamandaleshwar of Juna Akhada at Prayagraj, with Kerala media describing him as the first Malayali to hold the position in the Akhada.

MAR–APR 2025

Dialogue & outreach in Kerala

Publicly documented visits included Amritapuri Ashram and the Vivekananda Kendra Vedic Vision Foundation at Kodungallur, where discussions focused on future Dharma-related activities and service.

2026

Mahamagha & Bharathapuzha

Swamiji was publicly associated with the Mahamagha Mahotsavam at Thirunavaya and with plans to study and support the rejuvenation of the Bharathapuzha.
