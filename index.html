Premium Seva Pathways

★ Sacred service with deeper commitment.

Two structured forms of monthly seva connect devotees with the wider work of Shri Juna Akhada while supporting sannyasis, sacred centres and Akhada activities.

✦
MONTHLY SEVA

Sadhu Seva

₹128minimum / month

This initiative aims to increase participation among devotees from Kerala in Sadhu Seva, through a monthly contribution of at least ₹128 towards the Akhada’s Yati Puja Bhandaras, where food, clothing, dakshina and other offerings are provided to revered sannyasis in Kashi and other centres.

  • Participants become members of the Shri Bhagavadpada Seva Samiti.
  • Each participant is recognised as a Sadhu Sevak.
  • Those who inspire at least ten people to undertake Sadhu Seva are recognised as Distinguished Members of the Samiti.

BHAKTAMANDALI TRUSTEE

Trustee Membership

₹1,028/ month Guru Dakshina

Devotees who offer ₹1,028 per month as Guru Dakshina will become trustees from South India in the Akhada’s Bhaktamandali. Contributions support Akhada activities across the country, accommodation, travel and medical care for sannyasis, and administration of its ashrams and temples.

1,008trusteeships available
  • Trustees who bring at least two additional trustees and dedicate at least 10% of their time to Akhada service are recognised as Distinguished Members.
  • Trustees and Distinguished Members receive special arrangements within the Kumbh Mela township, with complimentary accommodation and meals.
  • An annual Maha Yajna will be conducted under Naga sannyasis at a sacred centre, with archana in the names of trustees and Distinguished Members and annual prasadam.
  • Once each year, members will also receive a rare Mahaprasadam, such as sacred water from the Amarnath Shiva Linga, a rudraksha offered in worship to Kashi Vishwanath, or a portion of Maa Kamakhya’s sacred ceremonial cloth.

Vision & Mission

Rooted in Dharma. Expressed through service.

Our purpose is to help Sanatana culture flourish through spiritual practice, cultural continuity, community strength and responsible care for sacred spaces and nature.

OUR VISION

A strong, living Sanatana community

To nurture a society in which Dharma is lived with understanding, sacred traditions are respected, communities support one another, and people participate actively in preserving spiritual and cultural heritage.

OUR MISSION

Bring people together for meaningful action

To coordinate spiritual learning, cultural activity, seva, environmental care and constructive community initiatives through ashrams, temples, kavus and other sacred spaces across Kerala.

Our Work

Serve sacred places, people and nature.

Through spiritual learning, selfless service and care for our natural and cultural heritage, we work towards stronger and more compassionate communities.

Serving Dharma, People, and Nature

Our Areas of Work

Shri Juna Akhada’s work in Kerala, guided by Shri Kalika Peetham, will bring people together through ashrams, temples and kavus for spiritual practice, community service, cultural learning and care for the natural environment.

01
✦

Care for sacred places

Support the cleanliness and responsible upkeep of temples, ashrams, kavus and pilgrimage sites, working with the people and institutions responsible for these places to help preserve their traditions.

02
⌁

Protect nature

Organize tree planting, forest conservation, and efforts to restore the health of rivers and ponds. Caring for nature supports the well-being of people and all living beings.

03
◈

Strengthen communities

Develop initiatives that support educational, social, health and economic self-reliance, with particular attention to people who need guidance and practical help.

04
ॐ

Encourage spiritual practice

Bring people together for namajapa, bhajans and satsangs at sacred places and in their homes.

05
△

Promote well-being

Encourage centres and programmes that bring together yoga, kalari and Ayurveda for physical and mental well-being.

06
✣

Serve travelling sannyasis

Help arrange food, accommodation and opportunities for satsang for travelling sannyasis, encouraging sadhu seva as a practice of hospitality and service.

07
✺

Preserve and share knowledge

Create opportunities to study and practise the scriptures, arts and other traditions rooted in Sanatana Dharma.

08
◇

Respond to concerns

Support respectful, lawful and constructive responses to concerns affecting Dharma, its institutions and spiritual teachers.
