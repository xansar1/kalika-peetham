Who We Are

An ancient tradition, serving today’s Kerala.

Shri Kalika Peetham is the South Indian arm of Juna Akhada. It leads and coordinates Juna Akhada’s activities in Kerala, bringing together people who wish to preserve Sanatana Dharma, strengthen their communities and take part in meaningful service.

Juna Akhada belongs to the ancient tradition of akhadas: communities of sannyasis who have dedicated their lives to the propagation and protection of Dharma. Its Naga sannyasis carry a long heritage of discipline, spiritual practice and commitment to their beliefs.

In Kerala, our activities will be centred around ashrams, temples and kavus — sacred groves. We aim to bring people together for spiritual learning, cultural activities and practical service. We also seek to listen to concerns facing the Hindu community and work towards thoughtful, constructive solutions.

We welcome everyone who believes in living by Dharma and wishes to see Sanatana culture flourish through active participation.
